Everything here can be edited, reordered or retired from the board in ✏️ Edit mode (see [How do parents manage the chore, good deed and reward lists?](issues/04-parent-screens.md)). **New items:** anything added with ✏️ Something else, or as a new reward, starts at **1 pebble** until someone changes it.

## How the numbers fit together

- Values are small (1–4) so a 4-year-old can count them.
- Doing **every daily chore** earns **10** a day. A realistic day for a kid is **5–8**, plus the odd good deed or occasional chore.
- So roughly: a ⭐ Sticker the same day, 🍦 Pick dessert in 1–2 days, 🎬 Movie night in about 4–5 days, 🎡 Day out in about 1½–2 weeks, 🧸 Big toy in about 2–3 weeks. Goals cover the bigger ones.
