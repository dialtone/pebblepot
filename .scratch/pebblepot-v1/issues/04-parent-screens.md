# How do parents manage the chore, good deed and reward lists?

Type: grilling
Status: resolved
Blocked by: 03

## Question

With no sign-ins and no approval steps, how and where do parents add, edit, reorder and retire daily chores, occasional chores, good deeds and rewards, and their pebble values? Is it a separate "parent mode" or just another screen? When a parent later strikes out a log entry, how do they find it and how is it shown? And what happens to old log entries when a chore or reward is edited or retired?

## Answer

The lists are managed **on the family board itself**. There's no separate parent area and no PIN.

- **"✏️ Something else" adds new items.** It's on **Today's jobs**, **Other jobs** and **Kind things**. It creates a new daily chore, occasional chore or good deed (name, picture, pebble value) and gives the tapped family member the pebbles in the same step. A **Keep on the board** toggle starts on; turning it off makes it a one-off that only appears in the log.
- **✏️ Edit mode.** A toggle at the top of the board puts a pencil on every row (name, picture, pebble value, daily or occasional) plus drag handles to reorder and a 🗑 **retire** button. Leaving edit mode returns to the normal board.
- **Rewards** follow the same pattern inside the Spend sheet: its own ✏️ Edit toggle and a "+ New reward" tile (name, picture, price).
- **Family members** follow the same pattern too. In edit mode, tapping a pot lets you add, rename, re-face, re-colour or retire a person. This settles the map's "Family member setup" item.
- **Anyone can edit**, kids included, with no PIN or authentication, matching the honour system.
- **The log keeps its own copies.** Every entry stores the name, picture and amount from when it happened, so editing or retiring an item never changes past entries or pots. Retired items leave the board but stay readable in the log.
- **Undo is same-day only and removes the entry.** A ✓, a count badge, the Undo toast and the ✕ on a person's today list all remove that entry from the log, but only on the day it was made. Once the day is over the log is permanent, so there's no "earlier days" fixing. This replaces the earlier idea of parents striking older entries, and the prototype's "↩︎ Put back" goes away. **Undo** and **Retire** are now in GLOSSARY.md.
