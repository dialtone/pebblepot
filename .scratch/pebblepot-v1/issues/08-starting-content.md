# What starting content does Pebblepot ship with?

Type: grilling
Status: resolved
Blocked by: 04

## Question

What does Pebblepot come with on first start? This covers the daily chores, occasional chores, good deeds and rewards, with their pictures and pebble values, and reward prices that make the economy feel right for a 4- and a 6-year-old (and for parents, who earn too). Everything is editable from the board afterwards (see [How do parents manage the chore, good deed and reward lists?](04-parent-screens.md)), so this is a sensible default to review, not a fixed list. It also covers what everyone's pots start at.

## Answer

The draft in [starting-content.md](../starting-content.md) is the default content, and the user went ahead to the spec without changing the lists:
- **Family members:** Kid 1 and Kid 2 (kids), Mom and Dad (parents). Faces and colours are placeholders, and pots start at 0.
- **9 daily chores** worth 10 pebbles a day in total, **11 good deeds**, **18 occasional chores** (values 1–4), and **17 rewards** (5–100, including 3 for grown-ups).
- **New items** start at 1 pebble.
- All of it is loaded once, on first start with an empty database, and is editable from the board afterwards.
